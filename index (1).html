About Multimedia Compression

Multimedia compression reduces the size of image, audio and video files while maintaining acceptable quality. It helps save storage space and reduces file transfer time.

Text

Replaces repeated characters and patterns with shorter codes — the basis of lossless formats like ZIP and GZIP.

Image

Reduces pixel redundancy and colour depth — formats like JPEG and WebP trade a little detail for a much smaller file.

Audio

Removes frequencies the human ear barely notices, so a compressed track still sounds close to the original.

Video

Combines image compression with motion prediction between frames to shrink large clips dramatically.

Animation

A sequence of frames played quickly — used here to visualise the compression pass itself as it runs.
